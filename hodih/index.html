How Often Does It Happen

An Android app to check gut feelings against reality

coming soon
Android

Sometimes a thought hits you: "this always happens," or "this never happens anymore." Usually you don't actually know. HODIH lets you check. Open a Case on something you think happens too often or not often enough, like noisy neighbors, the kids fighting, or a toddler's cold. Log it every time it happens, and let the app tell you once there's enough data: fact, or just a feeling.

Your privacy matters: everything lives on your phone. No accounts, no cloud, no analytics, and the app doesn't request network permission. Export and import your data any time.

What's in the app

  • Cases: open one for anything you've noticed and want to check, from noisy neighbors to your partner doing something nice unprompted. Turn on duration, intensity, or tags for it as needed.
  • Logging: one tap from a home-screen widget, or a detail sheet when you want to add more.
  • Per-case Insights: a dot timeline, a year-in-pixels calendar, a day-of-week × time-of-day rhythm heatmap, gaps, streaks, durations, intensity, and tag breakdowns.
  • Trends, found automatically: changes and patterns in your data, flagged only once there's enough evidence behind them.
  • The Big Picture: every Case on one shared calendar, so patterns across different parts of your life are easier to see.
  • Rules: set an expected rate for a Case and get notified once that's actually confirmed by the data.
  • Share a Case: a Summary card, an Insights card with the stats you pick, or a History card of your raw log, each previewed before you send it.
  • Three themes, three voices: Plain, Intense, and Bright change the colors and every word the app says.
  • Your data stays yours: HODIH sends nothing anywhere itself. Export as JSON, CSV, or PDF, and import your JSON back anytime. Android's own device backup can still carry your data off the phone if you have that turned on, with a Settings toggle to opt out.
